Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Health Pace (diet.talabatito.com, and the Health Pace mobile app for Android and iOS) is a health and nutrition platform. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect your username, email address, phone number, and health data you voluntarily provide such as weight, height, age, gender, body composition, and nutrition/activity goals. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from that provider. If you connect a wearable device, we receive the fitness/health data you authorize it to share (steps, heart rate, sleep, etc.). If you grant location permission, we use your approximate location only to show local weather and hydration recommendations.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide your personalized meal plan, calorie/activity targets, AI coaching, and to improve the product. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

Unlike a platform with no external dependencies, Health Pace relies on the following third-party services, each of which processes a limited, specific slice of data as described:

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with data brokers or for third-party advertising unrelated to measuring our own ad campaigns.

4. YouTube API Services

Our platform uses YouTube API Services to publish educational content on the DietHub Arabia channel. By using our services, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. You can review the Google Privacy Policy to understand how YouTube handles data. We use YouTube API data only for video publishing and do not store any YouTube user data.

5. Cookies & Similar Technologies

We use cookies/local storage to maintain your login session, remember your language preference, and remember dismissed in-app banners. Google Ads' measurement tag is loaded only on visits that arrive from a Google ad click.

6. Data Security

We apply industry-standard technical security measures — including password hashing, rate limiting, and encrypted connections — to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time (see Section 9); we delete your personal data immediately upon a verified request, except where retention is required by law (see Section 9 for exactly what that covers).

8. Children's Privacy

Health Pace is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Your Rights

You may view or edit your data at any time from the app's Settings/Profile screens. As of August 2026, you can delete your account yourself — Settings → Delete Account, after confirming your password — with no need to contact us first; it immediately removes your nutrition logs, weight history, lab results, wearable data, and AI suggestion history, and signs you out of every device. Two categories of records are kept regardless: payment/transaction history and security audit logs, retained for financial record-keeping and fraud-prevention purposes, not tied to your active account. You can also revoke Google/Facebook Sign-In access directly from your Google or Facebook account settings, or reach us at the contact below for anything the in-app tools don't cover.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or data requests: info@talabatito.com

6. أمن البيانات

نطبّق إجراءات أمان تقنية بمعايير الصناعة — بما في ذلك تشفير كلمات المرور، وتحديد معدل الطلبات، والاتصالات المشفّرة — لحماية بياناتك من الوصول غير المصرح به أو الفقدان أو الإفصاح.

7. الاحتفاظ بالبيانات

نحتفظ ببيانات حسابك طالما كان حسابك نشطاً. يمكنك حذف حسابك في أي وقت (انظر القسم 9)؛ نحذف بياناتك الشخصية فوراً عند طلب موثّق، باستثناء ما يقتضي القانون الاحتفاظ به (انظر القسم 9 لمعرفة ما يشمله ذلك بالضبط).

8. خصوصية الأطفال

Health Pace غير موجّه للأطفال دون سن 16 عاماً، ولا نجمع بياناتهم عن علم.

9. حقوقك

يمكنك عرض بياناتك أو تعديلها في أي وقت من شاشات الإعدادات/الملف الشخصي في التطبيق. اعتباراً من أغسطس 2026، يمكنك حذف حسابك بنفسك — الإعدادات ← حذف الحساب، بعد تأكيد كلمة المرور — دون الحاجة لمراسلتنا أولاً؛ وهذا يحذف فوراً سجلات تغذيتك وتاريخ وزنك ونتائج تحاليلك وبيانات جهازك القابل للارتداء وسجل اقتراحات الذكاء الاصطناعي، ويسجّل خروجك من كل الأجهزة. تُحفظ فئتان من السجلات بغض النظر عن ذلك: سجل المدفوعات/المعاملات المالية وسجلات التدقيق الأمني، وذلك لأغراض حفظ السجلات المالية ومنع الاحتيال، غير مرتبطة بحسابك النشط. يمكنك أيضاً إلغاء صلاحية تسجيل الدخول عبر Google/Facebook مباشرة من إعدادات حسابك في Google أو Facebook، أو مراسلتنا على العنوان أدناه لأي شيء لا تغطيه أدوات التطبيق.
